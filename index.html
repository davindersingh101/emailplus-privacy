Privacy Policy

EmailPlus, operated by Dipa Poros — Last updated: 17 November 2026

EmailPlus ("EmailPlus," "we," "us") is a rules-based inbox automation tool. This policy explains what data EmailPlus accesses, stores, and shares when you connect a Gmail account to it.

1. What EmailPlus accesses in your Gmail account

When you connect Gmail, EmailPlus requests the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • gmail.readonly — to read message metadata (sender, subject, date, a short snippet) and, where a rule requires it, message content, so your rules can be matched against real mail.
  • gmail.modify — to apply the outcome of a matched rule: adding/removing labels, archiving, or moving a message to Trash, and to create draft replies for your review.

EmailPlus does not request Gmail's send scope. It can create drafts; it cannot send mail on your behalf under any circumstance.

EmailPlus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. What we store

  • Your Gmail OAuth token — encrypted at rest, used only to make the API calls above on your behalf.
  • Your rules — the conditions and actions you configure (e.g. "label mail from X").
  • Processed message metadata — sender, subject, date, and a short snippet for messages a rule has acted on, kept so EmailPlus can show you a history and avoid re-processing the same message twice.
  • Optional API keys you supply — if you add your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google Gemini key for semantic matching, it is encrypted at rest and used only to call that provider on your behalf.

We do not store full message bodies or attachments beyond the short snippet needed to show you what a rule matched.

3. Who else sees your data

By default, no one. EmailPlus does not sell data, run ads, or share your information with third parties for marketing. The only exception is a feature you explicitly turn on yourself:

  • Semantic matching — if you add your own LLM API key and enable semantic rules, the relevant message metadata/snippet is sent to that provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google, depending on which key you added) to evaluate the match. That provider's own privacy policy governs how they handle that request. If you don't add a key or enable this feature, nothing is sent to an LLM provider.

4. Security

OAuth tokens and API keys are encrypted at rest before being written to disk. Server-side secrets used to run the service are kept in a managed secret store, not in source code or configuration files. Access to the server is restricted to the operator.

5. Your controls

  • You can disconnect Gmail at any time from EmailPlus's Settings page, or by revoking access directly from your Google Account permissions page. Either immediately stops EmailPlus from accessing your mailbox.
  • You can remove a stored LLM key at any time from Settings.
  • You can request deletion of all data EmailPlus holds about your account by contacting us at the address below.

6. Data retention

Rules, processed-message history, and tokens are kept for as long as your account is active, so the service can function. Disconnecting Gmail or requesting deletion removes the associated stored data.

7. Children's privacy

EmailPlus is not directed at children and is not knowingly used by anyone under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access or delete your data, can be sent to support@dipaporos.com.
